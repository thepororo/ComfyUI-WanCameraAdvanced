# Support Wan Camera Advanced

[English](SUPPORT.md) | [简体中文](SUPPORT.zh-CN.md) | [한국어](SUPPORT.ko.md)

Wan Camera Advanced is free, open-source software. Sponsorship is optional and
never required to install, run, or update the nodes.

## About the maintainer

I am a South Korea-based, self-taught independent developer. I maintain this
project without institutional or corporate backing. Sponsorship provides time
for testing, compatibility work, documentation, and maintenance.

## Sponsor development

Support development through [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/thepororo).
Funding is used for Wan and ComfyUI compatibility testing, camera-path
validation, example workflows, documentation, bug fixes, and improvements.

Sponsors do not receive a functionally restricted build. Development previews
or roadmap notes may be shared early when practical.

## Bugs, requests, and paid work

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/thepororo/ComfyUI-WanCameraAdvanced/issues)
for reproducible bugs and generally useful requests. Do not post private files,
credentials, API keys, or personal information.

Installation help, workflow tuning, and custom development are separate from
sponsorship. Scope, availability, price, and delivery terms must be agreed
before paid work begins. Sponsorship does not guarantee individual support.
